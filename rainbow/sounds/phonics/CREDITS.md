# Phonics sounds

Recorded phonics sounds (UK, Letters and Sounds Phase 2–3) from **buzzphonics**
by Debbie Dann — https://github.com/hellodeborahuk/buzzphonics — MIT License (c) 2022 Debbie Dann.
Converted from .m4a to mono 64 kbps .mp3. `oo` = long oo (moon, zoo), `ooo` = short oo (book, look) — the order of Letters and Sounds Phase 3.
